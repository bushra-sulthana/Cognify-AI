CAPITAL CITIES
Paris is the capital and largest city of France. It is located in the north of the country on the River Seine.

Canberra is the capital city of Australia. Canberra was purpose-built as the capital and was chosen as a compromise between the two largest cities. Sydney is the largest city of Australia by population, but Sydney is not the capital of Australia. Melbourne is the second largest city of Australia and served as the temporary seat of government until 1927, but Melbourne is not the capital either.

Tokyo is the capital of Japan. New Delhi is the capital of India, while Mumbai is the largest city of India by population. Ottawa is the capital of Canada; Toronto is the largest Canadian city. Brasilia is the capital of Brazil, not Rio de Janeiro or Sao Paulo. Ankara is the capital of Turkey, not Istanbul. Washington, D.C. is the capital of the United States of America. Wellington is the capital of New Zealand, while Auckland is its largest city.

RIVERS AND MOUNTAINS
The Nile in Africa and the Amazon in South America are the two longest rivers in the world, and different measurement methods place either one first. The Amazon carries the largest volume of water of any river.

Mount Everest is the highest mountain above sea level, with an elevation of about 8,849 metres. It lies on the border between Nepal and the Tibet Autonomous Region of China.

The Sahara is the largest hot desert in the world and covers much of North Africa. Antarctica is the largest cold desert.

OCEANS AND CONTINENTS
The Pacific Ocean is the largest and deepest ocean on Earth. The Mariana Trench in the western Pacific contains the deepest known point of the ocean floor.

There are seven continents in the commonly taught model: Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Europe, North America, Oceania (Australia) and South America. Asia is the largest continent by both area and population.

INDIA
Hyderabad is the capital of the Indian state of Telangana. It is known for the Charminar, built in 1591, and for its information technology district. Telangana was formed as a separate state on 2 June 2014.
