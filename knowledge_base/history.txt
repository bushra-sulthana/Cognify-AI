TWENTIETH CENTURY
The Second World War began in 1939 and ended in 1945.

The first human landing on the Moon took place on 20 July 1969 during the Apollo 11 mission of the United States. Neil Armstrong was the first person to walk on the Moon and Buzz Aldrin followed him, while Michael Collins remained in the command module.

India gained independence from British rule on 15 August 1947. Jawaharlal Nehru became the first Prime Minister of India, and Dr. Rajendra Prasad became the first President of India in 1950.

The Constitution of India came into effect on 26 January 1950, which is celebrated as Republic Day.

EARLIER PERIODS
The Taj Mahal in Agra, India, was commissioned by the Mughal emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal. Construction began around 1632 and was largely completed around 1653.

The printing press with movable metal type was developed in Europe by Johannes Gutenberg around 1440.

The Great Wall of China was built and rebuilt over many centuries; most of the wall that survives today was constructed during the Ming dynasty, between the fourteenth and seventeenth centuries.

SCIENCE HISTORY
Isaac Newton published "Philosophiae Naturalis Principia Mathematica" in 1687, setting out the laws of motion and universal gravitation.

Albert Einstein published his special theory of relativity in 1905 and the general theory of relativity in 1915. He received the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1921, awarded mainly for his explanation of the photoelectric effect rather than for relativity.
